# Starfolk privacy policy

Effective: 7 November 2026. Applies to Starfolk for Android (Google Play and F-Droid builds).

Short version: no account, no ads, no tracking. Everything you write or feel stays on your phone. The Google Play build can send a few anonymous usage events, but only if you say yes. The F-Droid build sends nothing at all.

## What stays on your phone, always

Your moods, notes, the names you give stars, unsent letters, Shelter journal entries and wellbeing check answers are stored only on your device, in a private database that is excluded from Android backup and device transfer. We can't see them, and nothing in the app reads or analyses what you write.

Your progress (completed quests, the sky, Shelter days walked) and settings are stored on your device and are included in your phone's own Android backup (to your Google account), so they survive a phone change. Starfolk itself never receives them.

## What can leave your phone

- Google Play build: anonymous usage events, only if you answer "Yes" when asked or turn on "Anonymous usage stats" in Settings. Off by default; turning it off deletes anything not yet sent. Events are event names with coarse values (for example "quest_completed: realm connect, level spark"), app version, Android major version and language. No device ID, no advertising ID, no account, no location, no free text, timestamps rounded to the hour. Optionally, only if you turn it on separately, your wellbeing score change as a range (for example "10 to 19").
- F-Droid build: nothing, ever. The F-Droid build has no internet permission.

The full list of events is shown in the app (Settings › Privacy › "See exactly what's sent"). Events are processed by Aptabase (EU region), a privacy-first analytics service, and kept for up to 12 months.

When you share a quest, the image and text go to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp). The image shows the quest only, never names, notes or moods. On Google Play, the store link includes "utm_source=share" so an install from a shared quest can be counted anonymously (only if the new user agrees to stats).

## Permissions

- Notifications (asked only when you choose a reminder): daily Spark and quest reminders. Lock-screen text is always neutral.
- Internet (Google Play build only): for the opt-in anonymous stats above.

No location, contacts, phone, camera, microphone or storage permissions. Crisis-line numbers are chosen from your SIM or language settings, which need no permission; calling opens your dialer.

## Your choices

- Turn stats on or off any time in Settings.
- Export everything (Settings › Your data › Export) as a file.
- Delete everything (Settings › Your data › Delete everything), or uninstall the app.

## Children

Starfolk is intended for adults (18+).

## Health information

Starfolk is for wellbeing and self-help. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

## Disclaimer: use at your own risk

- The information in Starfolk (quests, Shelter texts, insights, calming exercises and crisis-line numbers) is general self-help information. It may be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Don't trust it fully, and don't use it as your only source of help.
- Crisis-line numbers were checked against public sources on the date shown in the app, but numbers and hours change. Always confirm a number with a trusted local source. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
- Starfolk is not a substitute for a doctor, therapist or other professional. If you're struggling, please reach out to one.
- You use Starfolk at your own risk. It is provided "as is", without any warranty, as its licence (GNU GPL version 3, sections 15 and 16) also states.
