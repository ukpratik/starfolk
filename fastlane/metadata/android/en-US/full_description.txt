Do one human thing a day.

Starfolk gives you one small, real-world quest each day: text someone you've been meaning to, watch a sunset from somewhere high, try the first lesson of something you keep putting off, take a walk without headphones. Every quest you do lands in your own hand-made sky: friends become foil stars with their names in handwriting, adventures become postage stamps, purpose grows a flame.

★ QUESTS THAT GET YOU OFF AUTOPILOT
• One quest a day, picked for your mood and energy. Swap it twice if it doesn't fit.
• Four realms: People, Adventure, Purpose and Care.
• Hold to tear the ticket and pick a time: plans beat willpower.
• Stamp it when it's done, note how it felt, and see why it works: every quest is grounded in published psychology research.

★ THE SHELTER, FOR HARD TIMES
A quieter space for a breakup (21 days), a work storm or burnout (14 days), or a health scare (14 days). One small step a day, at your pace. Miss a day and nothing is lost; slip and you can start again, no shame. Unsent letters, a no-contact counter, a private journal.

★ HELP, ALWAYS ONE TAP AWAY
"Need help now?" is on every screen: a 60-second grounding exercise, messages to reach someone you trust, and crisis lines for your country.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account. No ads. Free.
• Your moods, notes, names, letters and journal stay on your phone. They're never sent anywhere, never analysed, and not even included in phone backups.
• Anonymous usage stats are off unless you say yes, and the app shows exactly what would be sent.

Starfolk is for wellbeing and self-help. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. If you're struggling, please talk to a professional. If you're in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.
